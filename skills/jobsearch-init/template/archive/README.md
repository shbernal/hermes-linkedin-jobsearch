# Archive

Ended opportunities.

- `pipeline/`: entries that became `rejected`, `dropped`, `closed` or `ghosted`, in the same format. Tracked in git, for the metrics.
- `applications/`: their drafts folders. Gitignored.

Closing out an entry, done by whichever step ends it:

- Move the pipeline file to `archive/pipeline/` and its `applications/` folder to `archive/applications/`. The company dossier and the `market/` posting stay.
- On LinkedIn, unsave the job, or archive it when it sits in the Applied tab, and archive its settled conversations, within the daily close-out cap in `workspace.yaml`. Past the cap, they wait for the next day.
- Archive its emails when `autonomy.archive_email` allows it.
