# Search criteria

What every track shares: what you're looking for, how to answer recurring form questions, and how jobs are scored. Each track adds its own keywords and fit points in `tracks/`. The facts about you live in `profile/`.

## Location

Where you are based, and which remote, hybrid and on-site arrangements work, from where.

## Contract

The contract types you accept, such as full-time employment, and the ones you don't.

## Level

Your seniority, the LinkedIn experience levels to search, and the years of experience above which a posting is skipped.

## Languages

The languages you work in, with your level in each.

## Companies

The kinds of company you target and the ones you rule out, and any company you want watched by name.

## Title exclusions

Words that disqualify a posting from its title alone.

## Standing answers

How to answer recurring form questions. Each answer names its date.

- Name: the name to use on resumes, forms and messages.
- Salary expectations: the figure or range to ask for, and how to justify it.
- Notice period and availability: when you can start.
- Work authorization: where you can work without sponsorship.
- Consent: contact for future openings and candidate-data retention, and the longest period you agree to.

## Fit

Hard filters a job must pass, then the points in its favor. Score 1 to 5 on what's left: 3 is worth researching, 5 is worth acting on the same day.

## Resume

How you like your resumes: sections, length, wording, what never appears. Guidance the agent follows unless an offer makes another choice better, and then says so. Each preference names its date.
